## Foreground

The complete V7 icon is preserved: all five sharp crown points, their exact positions and proportions, the slate protractor arc, white opening, five thin gold inward points, and pivot. It remains symmetric about x = 96. There are no rounded finials from V8.

## Rear layer

The second variant adds four darker gold points **behind** the unchanged foreground. They sit at −135°, −105°, −75° and −45°, between the original spike axes. Their 76 / 86 / 86 / 76 tip radii keep them smaller than the adjacent tallest foreground points. Their roots are hidden at radius 52 inside the existing crown, so the protractor is unaffected.
