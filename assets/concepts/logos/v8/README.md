## Crown

The radial spikes become a broad, continuous royal crown. Five upward points are joined by rising shoulders and softened valleys. Small round finials make the silhouette read as a crown. The center is tallest, the inner pair is lower, and the outer pair is lowest. Both sides mirror about **x = 96**.

The point centers are `(38, 64)`, `(67, 48)`, `(96, 36)`, `(125, 48)` and `(154, 64)`. Each has a 3-unit finial. Paired horizontal positions sum to 192, and every left-side curve has a mirrored right-side counterpart.

## Preserved exactly from V7

The slate radius-48 measurement arc, white radius-28 opening, all five thin gold graduation paths, and the gold pivot circle are copied without any geometry or styling changes. The lower gold radius-56 rim and its baseline are retained. Both original viewBoxes, the wordmark and all colors also stay the same.

Gold `#F5BD4F`; slate/navy `#253F68`; warm gold TOOLS text `#B67714`; white opening `#FFFFFF`. The surrounding canvas is transparent.
