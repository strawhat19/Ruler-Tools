# Ruler Tools logo concepts — v5

This round refines the crown-protractor idea in `v4/02-crown-protractor.svg`. The crown is centered on the protractor's pivot and has a symmetrical three-point silhouette. The earlier `v4` files remain untouched.

- `01-seated-crown-protractor.svg` fits the crown's lower edges behind the protractor's curved rim. The center point rises over the pivot, while both sides meet the arc at the same height. Intended for headers and wider brand lockups.
- `02-integrated-crown-protractor.svg` joins the crown and protractor into one navy outer silhouette. The inset gold measurement arc leaves a consistent navy edge around the sides. Intended for the same lockup, with a stronger icon silhouette at smaller sizes.

Both concepts use navy `#253F68`, gold `#F5BD4F`, warm gold `#B67714`, and white `#FFFFFF` on a transparent surrounding canvas. The SVGs remain editable.
