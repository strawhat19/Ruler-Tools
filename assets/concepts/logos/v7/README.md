A focused revision of [V6's five-point crown protractor](../v6/01-five-point-crown-protractor.svg).

- `01-symmetric-crown-protractor.svg` — full logo, with the V6 typography, positions, colors and 760 × 180 canvas.
- `02-symmetric-crown-protractor-icon.svg` — the same artwork on a compact, centered icon canvas.

## Requested changes

The crown follows one semicircle centered at `(96, 138)`, with a 56-unit outer rim. Its five points are equally spaced at 30° increments: −150°, −120°, −90°, −60° and −30°. There is also a 30° interval from each outermost point to its baseline end, distributing the crown over the entire curve.

Tip radii progress **70 → 83 → 95 → 83 → 70**. Relative to the rim, point heights are **14 → 27 → 39 → 27 → 14**. Each point has matching shoulders at ±10° from its axis. Short circular valley sections keep the silhouette continuous with the protractor curve.

Five gold inward graduations align with those same axes. Each tapers from a **1.8-unit root to a sharp point**, thinner than V6's 3-unit strokes. Roots begin at radius 50 and cross the navy arc's radius-48 boundary, making a 2-unit overlap with the gold crown. There is no gap. All tips end at radius 35.

The icon is bilaterally symmetrical about **x = 96**: crown tips, shoulders, circular valleys, inward points, opening and pivot all share the same center and mirrored geometry.
